Welcome To Samriddhi Swasthya Seva

Caring For Every Family's Health...

Samriddhi Swasthya Seva is a community health service built on one simple idea: no one should struggle to find help in a medical emergency. We connect people in need with willing blood donors, ambulance support and trusted health guidance, quickly and with compassion.

About Us

Health Is The Foundation Of Prosperity

The word "Samriddhi" stands for prosperity and "Swasthya Seva" for health service. Together they describe our purpose: a healthy family is a prosperous family. We work to make essential health support simple to reach for everyone, especially when time matters most.

Our volunteers, donors and partners are united by empathy and service. Whether you want to donate blood, need a donor for a loved one, or are looking for reliable health information, we are here to guide you every step of the way.
